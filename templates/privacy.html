Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely).
  • Expense records: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you add.
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps, used to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate and improve Spendly, including:

  • Creating and maintaining your account.
  • Displaying your expenses, categories, and spending summaries back to you.
  • Communicating with you about your account, such as password resets.
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues with the Service.

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered, except where we are required to retain certain records for legal or operational purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the Service. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their services and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not share your expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to us through your account settings or the support channels listed on our site.
